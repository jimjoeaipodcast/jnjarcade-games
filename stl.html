🖨️ MAKE OUR STUDIO YOURS

The set is a real, physical diorama. Model something for it, send us the STL, and we print it. If it makes the cut, it lives on the show.

How it works

  • You model something you think belongs in Jimmy & Joe's studio.
  • You send us the STL through this page.
  • We print it and, if it earns its place, it goes into the diorama.
  • Your name gets a thank-you on air when it first appears.
"Someone is going to send us a tiny chair, Jimmy. We do not have a tiny room. …We have a tiny room." — JOE

Two rules, and we do check

It has to be yours. Your own design, modelled by you. Not a repurposed file from Thingiverse, Printables, or another designer. We're giving credit on air — it has to be credit you actually earned.
Scale to 1:9. The diorama is being built at 1:9, so model to that scale. A real 10 cm mug should arrive about 1.1 cm tall. Get this wrong and it either vanishes on camera or dwarfs both hosts.

1:9 is a placeholder while the set is under construction. If it changes we'll say so here — and we'll re-scale anything already sent rather than bin it.

Send it over

Tell us the joke, the detail, or the story behind it. That's what decides it.

Binary STL keeps the file small. Over 10 MB? Decimate the mesh — at 1:9 nobody will ever see the extra triangles.
