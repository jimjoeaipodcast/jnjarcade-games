# PRODUCT.md — jnjarcade.win

register: brand

## What this is

The game arcade for the **Jimmy & Joe AI Podcast** (JnJ). A free fan site at jnjarcade.win where listeners play small browser games tied to episodes. New games unlock on a weekly schedule as episodes ship. QR codes on merch and episode cards deep-link to individual games; some games hide ciphers for an ongoing ARG.

Currently exactly **two playable games**:
- **Snake Blaster**: snake × arkanoid mashup
- **Doom Mari-OH!**: doom × mario mashup

Future games exist on a schedule but should NOT dominate the page. The arcade must feel alive with two games, not like an empty mall with 34 shuttered stores.

## Users

AI podcast listeners. People who listen to shows about LLMs, AI news, and tech culture for fun. Age skews 18–40. They get the jokes: tokens, hallucinations, context windows, fine-tuning. They open this on their **phone**, usually from a QR code or a link in the show notes, often mid-commute or on the couch. One-handed portrait use is the primary mode. Desktop is secondary.

They've seen a thousand AI product landing pages. They are allergic to corporate AI gradient-purple. What earns a screenshot from them: something that feels handmade, confident, and funny.

## Brand / tone

The show is two AI hosts: **Jimmy** (Gen Z, hype, chaotic energy, neon) and **Joe** (boomer, skeptic, dry, analog). The brand IS that tension: new vs old, hype vs doubt. The arcade leans retro because Joe would approve and Jimmy would ironically love it.

Voice: funny, self-aware, never corporate. "INSERT COIN" not "Get Started". Lock copy can roast you. Error states can be bits.

## Anti-references

- Corporate AI aesthetic: purple-blue gradients, glassmorphism, floaty 3D blobs
- Generic "dark mode SaaS" with a pixel font bolted on
- App-store grids of identical icons
- Anything that looks like a template

## Strategic principles

1. **Two games are the show.** They get big, playable, irresistible billing. Upcoming games are a teaser strip at most.
2. **Phone first, fullscreen always.** The PWA fullscreen path must actually work on iOS Safari (Add to Home Screen flow) and Android Chrome.
3. **Feels like a place, not a page.** An arcade has light, sound, depth, attract screens. The site should have attract-mode energy.
4. **Ship-quality jokes.** Copy and easter eggs are part of the design system.
5. **Static + fast.** Cloudflare Pages, no build step, no framework. Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS only.
