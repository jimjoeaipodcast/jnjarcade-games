🎨 FACE LAB

Jimmy and Joe's faces are LED dot grids. Draw them an expression — the best ones air on the show, and the legendary ones become part of their face forever.

How it works

  • Pick Jimmy or Joe, then tap dots on and off to build an expression.
  • Send it. Every submission lands on the studio board for review.
  • The best ones air on the show, credited to you.
  • A legendary one gets adopted permanently — it becomes part of that host's face, used in every episode after.
"They're letting the internet design my face. My actual face. I've seen what the internet does with power, Jimmy." — JOE

What makes one land

Read it at a glance. These are low-resolution LED panels on a diorama set, seen small and often in motion. An expression that needs studying doesn't survive the cut to camera.
Give it one clear feeling. Smug, appalled, delighted, dead inside. The face has to do a job in a scene — a shape with no emotion behind it has nowhere to be used.

Jimmy and Joe have different resting faces, so the same dots read differently on each. Try yours on both.

Ready?
