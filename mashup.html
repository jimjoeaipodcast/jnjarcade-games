🕹️ BUILD US A CABINET

Every cabinet in the arcade is two old games smashed together. Pitch us yours — if it makes the cut, we build it and it goes in the arcade with your name on it.

How it works

  • You pick two games nobody has ever put together.
  • You tell us the ONE twist that makes the combination sing.
  • We build it, playtest it, and it goes into the arcade.
  • Your name goes on the cabinet and gets a shout on the show.
"He wants to combine two games that already didn't work. I've seen this before, Jimmy. It was called the nineties." — JOE

What actually gets built

Two parents, one loop. Take the core loop from the parent that plays better on a phone, and the tools or theme from the other. Two loops fight each other — that's how you get a cabinet that feels like neither game.
One signature twist. Every cabinet here has exactly one: Hop-Man is Pac-Man pellets on a Frogger road. Pin-Vaders is a pinball table the invaders are marching down. One twist sings; three is mud.

Best pitches are specific. "Tetris × Bomberman" is a title. "Tetris where the lines you clear drop as bombs on the player below" is a cabinet.

Pitch it

Tell us the moment a player would screenshot. That's what decides it.
