Privacy Policy

Jimmy & Joe AI Podcast and JnJ Arcade (jnjarcade.win) · Last updated 28 September 2026

This policy explains what information we collect when you use jnjarcade.win, send us a submission, or watch our shows, and what we do with it. We keep it short because we collect very little.

What we collect

  • Submissions you send us: fan facts, product links, game ideas and files. The name, email address and content you type into a submission form are stored so we can review the submission and let you know if it is used on the show. Submissions are deleted automatically after 90 to 180 days, depending on the form.
  • High-score names: the name or initials you enter on an arcade leaderboard, shown publicly on that board.
  • Play counts: anonymous counts of how often each game is played. These are not linked to you.
  • Abuse protection: your IP address is used briefly to rate-limit submissions and stop spam. It is kept for no more than about an hour and is not used for anything else.
  • On your device: games save progress and settings in your browser's local storage. This stays on your device and is not sent to us.

We do not use advertising trackers or third-party analytics on jnjarcade.win, and we do not sell or rent your information to anyone.

Email

If you give us your email address with a submission, we may email you about that submission, for example to say it aired. Emails are sent from hello@jnjarcade.win using the email provider Resend, or from our Gmail account. To stop hearing from us, reply and say so.

Google and YouTube data

Our own tools use Google APIs, including the YouTube Data API, only to manage the Jimmy & Joe YouTube channel: uploading our episodes, editing their details and reading our channel's analytics. These tools access only our own accounts. They do not access, collect or store data from viewers' Google accounts.

Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. When you watch our videos on YouTube, Google's own privacy policy applies.

Payments and memberships

Payments and memberships are handled by third parties such as Patreon and Ko-fi. We never see or store your card details. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Hosting

jnjarcade.win is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes the technical request data needed to deliver the site to you.

Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete anything we hold about you, such as a submission or a leaderboard name. Email hello@jnjarcade.win and we will deal with it. If you are in the UK or EU, you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office).

Children

The arcade is fine for all ages, but the submission forms are not intended for children under 13. If a child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
